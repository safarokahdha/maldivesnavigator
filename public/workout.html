🏋️ 4-Day Workout

Sunday – Chest + Biceps

  • Bench Press — 4 × 6–10
  • Incline DB Press — 3 × 8–12
  • Chest Fly — 3 × 10–15
  • Push-ups — 2 sets
  • Barbell Curl — 3 × 8–12
  • Incline DB Curl — 3 × 10–12
  • Hammer Curl — 3 × 10–12

Monday – Back + Triceps

  • Lat Pulldown — 4 × 8–12
  • Seated Cable Row — 3 × 8–12
  • Single-arm DB Row — 3 × 10
  • Face Pull — 3 × 12–15
  • Pushdown — 3 × 10–12
  • Overhead Extension — 3 × 10–12
  • Dips — 2–3 sets

Tuesday – Legs

  • Squat/Leg Press — 4 × 8–12
  • Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 8–10
  • Walking Lunges — 3 × 10/leg
  • Leg Curl — 3 × 10–12
  • Leg Extension — 3 × 10–12
  • Calf Raises — 4 × 12–15

Wednesday – Shoulders + Abs + Cardio

  • Overhead Press — 4 × 8–10
  • Lateral Raise — 3 × 12–15
  • Rear Delt Fly — 3 × 12–15
  • Shrugs — 3 × 10–12
  • Hanging Leg Raises — 3 sets
  • Cable Crunch — 3 × 15
  • Plank — 3 × 45–60 sec
  • 20–30 min Cardio
Progression: If you can do all sets at the top of the rep range, increase the weight next workout. Example: 4 × 6–10 means 4 sets of 6–10 reps.
